Category
A / B
Micro-Objective
Standardize wheelchair clamp alignment (110°) and execute the official distance-setting protocol (mesurage) within 45 seconds without posture distortion or seat detachment.
Frame Angle
110° (Rule o.100)
Measure Point
Elbow Crease (o.102)
Holding Bar
Level w/ Lower Ribs
3-Phase Ladder (10 Min Station) Check → Reach → Simulation
1
Static Rigging Check (3m): Verify frame clamps are fully locked. Ensure knees flexed 90°–100°, feet strapped, pelvis seated. [3x reach checks]
2
Postural Re-Centering (3m): Full extension attack to target, recover to En Garde. Return to neutral spine without leaning on side-guard. [8 reps]
3
Referee Countdown Simulation (4m): Step into frame, verify distance under 30s referee clock, lock pins, salute, En Garde. [3 bout resets]
● Red Flags (Faults)
  • • Leaning forward during measure to steal distance.
  • • Holding bar above sternum (impinges shoulder).
✓ Optimal Mechanics
  • • Lumbar stays anchored flat against backrest.
  • • Wheels seated flush in clamps with zero play.
Kinesthetic Feel:
"Root your sit-bones into the cushion during measurement. Never stretch forward to fake reach. Set honest distance so your blade mechanics stay pure."

Holding-Bar Kinetic Push-Pull Mechanics
Italian Federscherma (+18% Tip Velocity) & Chinese Core Pre-Activation
Category
A / B
Micro-Objective
Replace passive holding-bar gripping with an active push-pull kinetic coupling: pulling backward to launch forward acceleration and pushing forward to brake defensive recoveries.
Holding Grip
Thumb Up, 45° Elbow
Grip Tension
4/10 Prep → 8/10 Hit
Torque Transfer
+18% Tip Velocity
3-Phase Ladder (12 Min Station) Launch → Brake → Double Action
1
Pull-to-Launch (4m): From En Garde, violently pull backward on holding bar while extending sword arm into direct thrust. Bar pull accelerates sword shoulder. [8 reps]
2
Push-to-Brake (4m): From maximum forward reach, violently push forward against bar to brake momentum and snap torso back to upright defense without hip slip. [8 reps]
3
Double-Action Exchange (4m): Coach shows high line → Athlete pulls bar to attack. Coach counters immediately → Athlete pushes bar to retreat into Parry 4. [6 cycles]
● Red Flags (Faults)
  • • White-knuckle grip causing neck/trap spasm.
  • • Non-sword hand releasing bar (Rule o.101 fault).
✓ Optimal Mechanics
  • • Audible exhale synchronized with thrust.
  • • Smooth torque transfer from holding arm to tip.
Kinesthetic Feel:
"The holding bar is your gas pedal and your brake pedal. Pull it toward your armpit to shoot forward; push it away toward the floor to snap backward."

3-Phase Ladder (10 Min Station) Boundary → Reach → Audit

Hungarian Szekeres Doctrine • Drawing Direct Attacks Short
Category
A / B
Micro-Objective
Induce opponent's attack to miss by 2–3 inches via rapid backward torso flexion (retaining Rule o.101 seat contact), followed by immediate counter-thrust into open preparation.
Frame Angle
110° (Rule o.100)
Measure Point
Elbow Crease
Holding Bar
Primed to Pull
3-Phase Ladder (12 Min Station) Timing → Deception → Priority
1
Predictable Timing (4m): Partner attacks on 3s count. Pull bar, lean torso back 15°–20°. Point misses by 2". Return straight thrust. [8 reps]
2
Broken Rhythm (4m): Partner varies cadence and feints. Athlete holds ground on feint, dodges on committed extension, hits low line. [10 reps]
3
Bout Scenario (4m): Tied 4–4. Partner has priority. Athlete can only score on dodge-and-hit within 1 tempo of opponent's miss. [5 touches]
● Red Flags (Faults)
  • • Buttock lifts off seat on lean (o.101 card).
  • • Sword elbow collapses downward or back.
✓ Optimal Mechanics
  • • Torso shifts just enough to clear tip (2").
  • • Weapon tip stays primed and aimed forward.
Kinesthetic Feel:
"Don't dodge with your sword arm; dodge with your chest. Pull the target back like a matador, keeping your point cocked and ready to fire forward."

Second-Intention False Preparation & Counter
Russian Tactical Model (Lesgaft: >64% Seated Touches)
Category
A / B
Micro-Objective
Execute an intentional false direct attack to draw opponent's parry or counter-attack, immediately parry their expected riposte, and score on the counter-riposte.
Tactical Rationale
Provoke Parry
First Action
False Half-Thrust
Final Touch
Counter-Riposte
3-Phase Ladder (12 Min Station) Draw → Cadence → Constraint
1
False Attack Draw (4m): Athlete launches half-extension false thrust into Sixte. Partner parries 6 and ripostes. Athlete catches riposte in opposition Parry 4 and hits. [8 reps]
2
Cadence Shift (4m): Athlete varies false attack speed. Partner delays riposte; athlete must hold parry position until riposte launches, avoiding premature twitch. [8 reps]
3
Pressure Game (4m): Live bout constraint: attacker can ONLY score via second-intention counter-riposte. Hits on direct first attacks do not count. [First to 3 touches]
● Red Flags (Faults)
  • • Overcommitting body on first attack (stuck).
  • • Telegraphing false attack by looking away.
✓ Optimal Mechanics
  • • First attack looks 100% real to defender.
  • • Attack → Parry → Riposte in one fluid tempo.
Kinesthetic Feel:
"The first attack is bait on a hook. Cast the bait smoothly. When the fish bites (the parry), set the hook with your second parry and finish cleanly."

Take-Home Guide
Second-Intention Practice at Home
Personal Coach Translation & Two-Target Wall Work
Target
Coaches
1. Able-Bodied Sparring Partner Drill
Seat an able-bodied teammate opposite you in a heavy stationary chair. Instruct them: "Whenever I extend, parry my blade as hard as you can and riposte straight at my chest." This gives your partner a simple job while giving you realistic second-intention counter-riposte practice.
2. Solo Two-Target Wall Drill
Place Target A (high outside) and Target B (high inside) on a wall. Strike Target A lightly (false draw), snap weapon back to guard (simulating parry), and strike Target B with maximum speed and extension. Perform 3 sets of 12 reps.
3. Compensatory Symmetry (Lesgaft Protocol)
• Eccentric Forearm Extensions: 3 sets of 12 with 2 lb weight to offload lateral epicondyle from heavy consecutive parries.
• Chest Doorway Stretch: Open anterior chest wall after tight second-intention defensive holding postures.
Coach Evaluation & Athlete Self-Audit Dual-Track
Coach Watch: Verify first false attack displays convincing extension drawing opponent's parry without overcommitting body. Confirm weapon tip re-engages target immediately upon counter-parry.
Coach Cues: "Sell first attack!" "Don't drown in reach!" "Counter-parry & fire!"
Athlete Self-Audit (How to Conduct): Pause after the phrase. Replay the sequence in your head. Did your preparation provoke a committed defensive response, and did you stay centered enough to execute the counter-action?
