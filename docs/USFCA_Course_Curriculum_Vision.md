## 1. Executive Summary

Wheelchair fencing in the United States stands at a pivotal developmental crossroads. While high-performance national camps gather our top talent periodically, **over 85% of wheelchair fencers train in local, able-bodied fencing clubs** where a certified para-fencing master is rarely present on a daily basis. Local personal coaches and able-bodied training partners (*partenaires valides*) want to support their seated athletes, but they face a critical information vacuum:
* Lack of standardized rigging, chair angle, and Rule o.102 *mesurage* (distance) protocols.
* Ambiguity regarding legal reaching thresholds and buttock contact integrity (Rule o.101/t.101 *décollement* rules).
* Absence of practical sparring guidelines that teach able-bodied partners how to present realistic tactical cues from a stationary chair without corrupting the wheelchair fencer's spatial schema.

To solve this, we initiated a field-tested project for the September 23, 2026 High-Performance Para-Fencing Camp: **The Parafencing Modular Lesson Card System**. Rather than remaining an ad-hoc collection of camp drills, this framework is specifically engineered to evolve into a **comprehensive, accredited USFCA Para-Fencing Coaching Course**, directly supporting the USFCA's active push to complete the **Level 4 (Prévôt / Bravo)** curriculum.

## 2. From Ad-Hoc Drills to a Comprehensive Certification Course

Traditional sports education often relies on dense, static manuals that sit on club shelves. In contrast, this curriculum adopts a **modular building-block model** grounded in the classical French *Fiche Pédagogique* (Pedagogical Card) standard developed at INSEP and the Fédération Française Handisport (FFH):

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                  USFCA CERTIFICATION LADDER INTEGRATION                 |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Level 2: Assistant Monitor (Entry)   -> Safety, Rigging, Strapping      |
| Level 3: Monitor (Intermediate)      -> Seated Lines, Kinetic Push-Pull |
| Level 4: Prévôt / Bravo (Competitive)-> Distance Traps, Second-Intention|
| Level 5: Fencing Master              -> Systems Design & Master Thesis  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Why Modular Cards Outperform Static Textbooks
1. **Strip-Side Operational Utility (Side A)**: Floor coaches at a busy camp or club do not have time to read paragraphs. Each card gives them an instantaneous 3-Phase Station Ladder (Check -> Reach -> Scenario), clear biomechanical angles, red-flag visual faults, and single-syllable verbal cues (*"Économie de mots"*).
2. **The Home-Club Translation Bridge (Side B)**: The reverse side transforms the camp drill into a take-home lesson for the personal coach and able-bodied club mates. It provides concrete instructions on how to replicate the drill using a standard stationary chair, wall targets, and solo elastic resistance bands.
3. **Closed-Loop Athlete Self-Regulation**: Side B equips the athlete with immediate post-phrase self-audit questions (e.g., *"Did my rear hip stay pinned during maximum extension?"*), fostering kinesthetic autonomy.

## 3. Curriculum Architecture: The 6 Core Modules

The curriculum is structured across six progressive modules designed to seamlessly slot into the USFCA Coach Education pathway:

### Module 1: Foundational Rigging, Chassis Biomechanics & Safety
* **USFCA Level Alignment**: Level 2 (Assistant Monitor) & Level 3 (Monitor)
* **Key Topics**:
  * Saint-Hilaire frame setup and rigid clamping (Rule o.100).
  * Wheelchair wheel camber locking and 110-degree backrest alignment.
  * The Rule o.102 *mesurage* protocol (bent forearm crease to guard center).
  * Safe athlete transfer protocols and core stability strapping.
  * Buttock contact integrity (Rule o.101/t.101) to eliminate yellow-card penalties.
* **Flagship Cards**: `MLC-FND-01` (Rigging Geometry), `MLC-FND-03` (Buttock Grounding).

### Module 2: The Non-Weapon Arm Kinetic Chain & Dynamic Balance
* **USFCA Level Alignment**: Level 3 (Monitor) & Level 4 (Prévôt)
* **Key Topics**:
  * Active push-pull coupling on the holding bar (Italian Federscherma / Chinese models).
  * Pulling the bar to accelerate forward shoulder extension (+18% blade tip velocity).
  * Violently pushing the bar to brake forward momentum without pelvic slide.
  * Prevention of Rule o.101 holding-bar releases during explosive actions.
  * Compensatory physical symmetry exercises (Lesgaft protocol) to balance asymmetric rotational torque.
* **Flagship Cards**: `MLC-FND-02` (Holding-Bar Push-Pull Mechanics).

### Module 3: Seated Spatial Geometry & Classical Blade Lines
* **USFCA Level Alignment**: Level 3 (Monitor)
* **Key Topics**:
  * High-inside (Line 4 / Quarte), high-outside (Line 6 / Sixte), low-inside (Line 7 / Septime), and low-outside (Line 8 / Octave) from a stationary chassis.
  * Eliminating whole-shoulder swinging in favor of finger-and-forearm point control.
  * Neuromuscular reaction time drills (under 250ms execution windows).
* **Flagship Cards**: `M1-02` (Target Quadrants & Seated Blade Lines).

### Module 4: Seated Distance Manipulation & Evasion (*Távolságérzék*)
* **USFCA Level Alignment**: Level 4 (Prévôt / Bravo)
* **Key Topics**:
  * Hungarian Szekeres doctrine: understanding distance when chairs are bolted to the floor.
  * Micro-distance torso dodging: drawing opponent attacks short by 2 to 3 inches via spinal flexion.
  * Keeping the sword point primed and aimed forward while evading with the chest.
  * Immediate single-tempo counter-thrusts into open preparation.
* **Flagship Cards**: `MLC-DST-01` (Micro-Distance Torso Dodge).

### Module 5: Second-Intention Tactical Bout Construction
* **USFCA Level Alignment**: Level 4 (Prévôt / Bravo) & Level 5 (Master)
* **Key Topics**:
  * Russian tactical model: over 64% of high-level seated touches are scored on preparation or counter-time.
  * Executing convincing false direct attacks to draw the opponent's committed parry.
  * Holding defensive position through cadence changes; avoiding anticipatory twitching.
  * Counter-ripostes and opposition parries executed from a rooted pelvic base.
* **Flagship Cards**: `MLC-TAC-01` (Second-Intention False Preparation).

### Module 6: Club Integration & Transferability to Standing Fencing
* **USFCA Level Alignment**: Universal Coach Elective / Continuing Education Units (CEU)
* **Key Topics**:
  * **Able-Bodied Sparring Partner Protocols**: Clear rules for club mates sitting in stationary chairs (fixed feet, honest distance, presenting realistic blade lines).
  * **Cross-Discipline Transferability**: How seated training accelerates standing fencers' infighting, point precision, and core torque by eliminating footwork compensation.
